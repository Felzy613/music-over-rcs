1. Connect Google Messages

  1. Press Start login below.
  2. Open messages.google.com/web in a private browser window, with developer tools open on the Network tab, and begin signing in as the bridge's reply says.
  3. Copy the request it names with Copy as cURL and paste it in the box. Press Send pasted request.
  4. Tap the emoji the bridge shows in Google Messages on your phone.

The pasted request holds your Google session cookies, as sensitive as your password. Paste it only here. It is deleted from the chat a minute after you send it; close the private window when you are done.

2. Your "message yourself" chat

Text yourself once from your phone. The bot finds that chat by itself (the chat with your own number) and starts answering in it within a minute or two. Only if it doesn't, enter your number here and press Find.

3. Save songs to OneDrive

Every song the bot sends is also saved, tagged and sorted as Artist / Album / 01 Title, in the app's own OneDrive folder (Apps/…). The bot cannot see the rest of your OneDrive.
